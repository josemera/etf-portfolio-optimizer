Walk-Forward
Out-of-Sample Simulation
? Simulates actually following the optimizer: each January it re-optimizes on the trailing lookback window, holds that allocation out-of-sample for 12 months, and repeats — chaining the results into one equity curve compared against benchmarks. Fixed universe starts at the first January where every selected ETF has full lookback history. Dynamic universe starts at the first January with at least 2 eligible ETFs whose universe is stable through the hold year (a mid-year joiner rounds the start forward to the next January); ETFs join as their history matures.
Optimize on trailing data, hold the result for a year, repeat — the honest test of whether the optimizer's picks carry forward out-of-sample.
